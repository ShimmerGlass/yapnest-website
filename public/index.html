Yapnest

A chat app for iPhone and Mac, built on XMPP.

  • Private. Messages are end-to-end encrypted with OMEMO.
  • Open. It works with any XMPP server. You choose where your account lives.
  • No tracking. No ads, no analytics, no trackers.

Yapnest is a client. It does not host your account or your messages: they are on the XMPP server you sign in to.
