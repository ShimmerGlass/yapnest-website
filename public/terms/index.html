What Yapnest is

Yapnest is a client for XMPP, an open messaging standard. It lets you sign in to an account on an XMPP server and chat. Yapnest does not host your account or your messages. Your server does.

If you break these rules, expect your account to be reported to its server’s operator, who can remove your content or close the account, and the authorities to be told where the law requires it.

Block and report

You can block anyone in the app. A blocked person can no longer reach any of your devices. When you block someone, you can also report them as spam or abuse. If your server takes reports, its operators get the report, which points to that person’s recent messages so they can find them on the server. The report itself does not contain the messages.

You can also write to support@yapnest.fr. See the support page. We aim to read reports within 24 hours.

Messages can be end-to-end encrypted, so we cannot read them. To report one, pass it on to us or to your server’s operator.
